Connects your WordPress and WooCommerce website to GabbarInfo AI for automated Google Ads tracking (gtag.js), Meta Pixel, dynamic purchase conversions, and autonomous AI SEO.

== Description ==
GabbarInfo AI Connect links your WordPress website directly with the GabbarInfo AI marketing agent. 

Key Features:
* **Automated Google Ads & Gtag.js Injection:** Injects official gtag.js scripts into your header without editing theme files.
* **Dynamic WooCommerce Conversion Tracking:** Automatically tracks purchases on the thank-you page with exact order ID, total revenue, and currency.
* **Form Lead Tracking:** Auto-detects Contact Form 7, WPForms, and Elementor Form submissions as Google Ads conversion leads.
* **Meta Pixel Integration:** Automatically triggers PageView, Lead, and Purchase events for Facebook Ads.
* **Autonomous SEO & Blogging:** Secure REST API endpoints allow GabbarInfo AI to publish SEO-optimized articles and update Yoast / Rank Math meta tags.

== Installation ==
1. Upload `gabbarinfo-connect.zip` to the `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install directly through WordPress Plugins.
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
3. Go to **Settings ➔ GabbarInfo AI** in your WordPress dashboard to view your AI Pairing Key or connect with your GabbarInfo AI agent.
