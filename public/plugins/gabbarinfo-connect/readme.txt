Connects your WordPress and WooCommerce website to GabbarInfo AI for automated Google Ads tracking (gtag.js), Meta Pixel, dynamic purchase conversions, autonomous AI SEO, and high-speed Ephemeral Media Bridge.

== Description ==
GabbarInfo AI Connect links your WordPress website directly with the GabbarInfo AI marketing agent. 

Key Features:
* **Ephemeral Media Bridge:** High-performance local hosting media staging for Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube video/image rendering with automatic immediate post-publish purge and hourly 60-minute cleanup.
* **Automated Google Ads & Gtag.js Injection:** Injects official gtag.js scripts into your header without editing theme files.
* **Dynamic WooCommerce Conversion Tracking:** Automatically tracks purchases on the thank-you page with exact order ID, total revenue, and currency.
* **Form Lead Tracking:** Auto-detects Contact Form 7, WPForms, and Elementor Form submissions as Google Ads conversion leads.
* **Meta Pixel Integration:** Automatically triggers PageView, Lead, and Purchase events for Facebook Ads.
* **Autonomous SEO & Blogging:** Secure REST API endpoints allow GabbarInfo AI to publish SEO-optimized articles and update Yoast / Rank Math meta tags.

== Installation ==
1. Upload `gabbarinfo-connect.zip` via WordPress Admin -> Plugins -> Add New -> Upload Plugin.
2. If already installed, click "Replace current with uploaded" to update in 5 seconds (all your pairing keys and settings are permanently preserved).
3. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress.
4. Go to **Settings ➔ GabbarInfo AI** in your WordPress dashboard to view your AI Pairing Key or connect with your GabbarInfo AI agent.
